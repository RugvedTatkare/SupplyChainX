# Data provenance and interpretation

SupplyChainX is a demonstrator, not a live supply-chain feed.

## Data categories

1. **Historical shipment outcomes** — retrospective observations used to explore delay and delivery patterns. These data are not themselves a forward-looking prediction.
2. **Synthetic supplier entities and warehouse mappings** — demo entities created to illustrate how shipment behaviour can be grouped operationally.
3. **Synthetic inventory snapshots/scenarios** — constructed values used to show inventory exposure and scenario handling.

Use the exact source and licensing notes that accompany any dataset file before redistributing it. Do not remove provenance labels when generating screenshots, charts, or exports.

## Risk scores

Risk bands and thresholds used in the demo are calibration choices for the available cohort. They are not external benchmarks, audited supplier ratings, or certified industry standards. Explain component scores and limitations when presenting a high-risk flag.

## Responsible interpretation

- A historical delay is evidence about a past shipment, not proof that a future shipment will be late.
- Correlation or a composite score should not be described as causation.
- Synthetic records must not be presented as actual company, supplier, or warehouse records.
- Do not claim predictive accuracy until a prospective or properly held-out evaluation has been run and documented.
